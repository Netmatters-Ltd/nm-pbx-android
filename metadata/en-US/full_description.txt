For use by existing customers of the NMPBX phone system.

This app provides:
- Voice over IP (VoIP) to make and receive calls through your NMPBX system.
- Manage your availability status in the NMPBX system
- View the availability of other users.
- Access to the central address book of contacts for your organisation.

See https://nmpbx.co.uk for more information.
